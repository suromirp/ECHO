ECHO

EDI message inspector v1.0 · July 2026

ECHO helps you quickly inspect and compare EDI messages. It highlights key information such as EANs, quantities, SSCCs and delivery references, and lets you trace results back to the original message.

What do the statuses mean?
MatchThe compared values are the same.
DifferenceThe compared values differ — worth checking.
CheckA minor difference — the important values still match.
Not applicableThis field isn't sent on either side, so it can't be compared.
InfoAn expected, soft difference — not counted as a mismatch.
No SSCC — can't matchThis pallet has no SSCC, so it can't be matched.
No match on other sideThis SSCC wasn't found on the other side.
OKNo structural issue found in this message.
⚠Worth a closer look — not necessarily an error.
ⓘExpected, non-problematic behaviour.
